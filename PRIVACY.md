# Privacy policy

**Solar**, for iPhone and iPad. Published by SOBYTES LTD (United Kingdom).
Last updated 16 November 2026.

## The short version

Solar collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising,
no third-party SDKs and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is recorded,
and nothing about you is sent anywhere.

This is a property of how the app is built rather than a promise about how it is operated.
Every number it shows is computed on your device from tables compiled into the app, and the
app has no server of its own to send anything to.

## What leaves your device

One thing, and only when you ask for it.

The **Surface** screens show real photographs taken from the surfaces of the five worlds
ever photographed from their own ground: Venus, Earth, the Moon, Mars and Titan. Those images are fetched over HTTPS from NASA's public image
library (`images-assets.nasa.gov`) at the moment you open the screen, because they are far
too large to ship inside the app. NASA receives an ordinary web request — the same one your
browser would make if you opened the photograph yourself. It carries no identifier we attach,
no account, and nothing about what else you have looked at. NASA's own privacy policy governs
what it does with its web server logs: <https://www.nasa.gov/privacy/>

Everything else — the positions of 32 bodies, their rotations, the planet imagery, the Moon's
elevation model, the star catalogue — is already inside the app and is read from your device.
Solar works with no network connection at all, apart from those photographs.

## What is stored on your device

A single preference, `solar.apiHost`, which exists so a developer can point a debug build at a
local server. It is not set in the App Store build and contains nothing about you. There is no
other stored state: Solar does not remember what you looked at between launches.

## Children

Solar is rated 4+ and is safe for children. It has no chat, no user-generated content, no
links out other than to NASA and to the scientific sources behind the figures, and no
purchases of any kind.

## Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete,
and no request you could make that we could act on. We mention this because "we take your
privacy seriously" usually means the opposite, and the honest statement here is that the
question does not arise.

## Changes

If a future version of Solar ever collects anything, this page will say so before that version
ships, and the App Store privacy label will change with it.
